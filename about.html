About

Building digital infrastructure for agricultural cooperatives

AgriCoop Digital Labs is focused on the digital transformation of India's agricultural cooperative ecosystem — connecting PACS, mandis, cooperative banks, and farmers through an integrated, offline-first platform.

Our Mission

Digitize. Connect. Include.

To transform India's agricultural cooperative ecosystem through digital infrastructure that works in real rural conditions — enabling financial inclusion, operational efficiency, and transparent market access.

PACS Digitization

Replacing manual ledgers with offline-first ERP systems for membership, loans, procurement, and accounting.

APMC Mandi Digitization

Digitalizing mandi operations from e-auctions to weighbridge, e-gates, payments, and e-NWR integration.

Rural Credit Access

Automating KCC origination and connecting cooperative banks with structured lending data from the field.

Operational Efficiency

Streamlining workflows across the cooperative ecosystem with integrated digital systems and automation.

Financial Inclusion

Bringing digital credit, payments, and market access to grassroots institutions and farmers.

Offline-First Technology

Architecture designed for intermittent rural connectivity with store-and-forward synchronization.

Pilot Strategy

A 12-month strategic pilot

A phased approach to validate the platform across PACS clusters and mandis, with targets aligned to the ecosystem opportunity.

Phase 01

PACS Cluster Pilot

Deploy the offline-first ERP across PACS clusters to validate cooperative operations and CBS synchronization.

Target: 200–3500 PACS
Phase 02

Mandi Digital Pilot

Implement the Mandi Digital Suite across mid-size mandis to validate e-auction, weighbridge, and payment flows.

Target: 133 mid-size mandis
Phase 03

Integration Pilot

Validate ecosystem integrations including eNAM, ONDC, e-NWR, and cooperative bank CBS connectivity.

Target: 233 districts
Phase 04

Scale Preparation

Refine the platform based on pilot learnings and prepare for ecosystem-wide scaling through partnership channels.

Target: Scale readiness

Pilot targets are strategic objectives, not guaranteed outcomes.
